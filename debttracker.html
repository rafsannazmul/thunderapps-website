About DebtTracker

DebtTracker turns your debts into a clear plan and a real debt-free date. Add your credit cards, loans and Buy Now Pay Later balances in seconds, and DebtTracker builds your payoff plan, shows exactly when you'll be debt-free, and moves that date closer with every payment you log.

It's private by design: no bank login, no account and no ads. Your numbers stay on your iPhone, and optional iCloud sync uses your own private iCloud database.

  • No bank login
  • No account, no ads
  • Data stays on your iPhone
  • 20 languages

What you can do

  • Your debt-free date

    Add credit cards, loans and Buy Now Pay Later balances in seconds and see exactly when you'll be debt-free.

  • Snowball vs Avalanche

    Pay the highest interest first to save the most, or the smallest balance first for quick wins. Compare both on your own numbers.

  • Payoff Time Machine

    Slide to add a little extra each month and watch your date and interest saved update instantly. Plan for refunds, bonuses and raises too.

  • What-if Lab

    Test a consolidation loan or a 0% balance transfer before you sign, and see whether it really saves you money.

  • IOUs with friends

    Record who owes whom for dinners, rent and family loans, and send a polite reminder in one tap.

  • Stay on track

    Gentle due-date and payday reminders, plus milestones and shareable progress cards to keep you going.

  • Widgets and Siri

    Keep your progress in view with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and check in with Siri.

  • DebtTracker PRO

    Unlimited debts and IOUs, custom strategies, 0% promo rates, statement scan, payment calendar, PDF reports, AI Debt Coach and CSV export.
