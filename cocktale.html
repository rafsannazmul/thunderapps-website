About Cocktale

Looking for cocktail recipes, mocktail recipes, or non-alcoholic drink ideas? Cocktale helps you discover drinks for every occasion while giving you an easy way to track your alcohol consumption.

Whether you're a cocktail enthusiast, a mocktail lover, or simply want to be more mindful of your drinking, Cocktale gives you a simple way to explore, save, and track your drinks.

  • Classics and new drinks
  • Mocktails and alcohol-free ideas
  • Personal drink tracker
  • New recipes added regularly

What you can do

Cocktail recipes

Discover recipes across different spirits, flavors and drink styles. Find the classics, explore new drinks and save the ones you love.

Mocktail recipes

Delicious mocktails and non-alcoholic drinks for when you want great flavor without the alcohol.

Consumption tracker

Keep a personal record of the drinks you have and build awareness of your drinking habits.

Discover by preference

Browse by type, flavor, spirit and occasion to find exactly what you're in the mood for.

Save favorites

Keep your favorite cocktail and mocktail recipes one tap away.

Collections

Organize drinks into collections that make sense for you: party menus, summer drinks, weeknight classics.

Share recipes

Send your favorite recipes to friends and family.

Fresh inspiration

New recipes arrive regularly, so there's always something worth trying.
