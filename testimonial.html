Inside our apps

A closer look at what you can do in each of our iPhone apps.

What you can do

  • DebtTracker

    See the exact day you'll be debt-free

    Add your cards and loans, pick Snowball or Avalanche, and watch every payment pull your date closer.

    Snowball & Avalanche
  • DocSnapper

    Turn a crumpled receipt into a clean PDF

    Point your camera and DocSnapper finds the edges, flattens the page and cleans it up, ready to save or send.

    18 cleanup filters
  • Cocktale

    Mix something new from your home bar

    Browse cocktails by spirit, flavor and occasion, save your favorites and group them into collections.

    Cocktails & mocktails
  • FlirtWise

    Open with something better than “hey”

    Drop in a profile or prompt and get openers you can send, each with a note on why it works.

    3 openers per profile
  • WealthWise

    See where your money goes

    Log your spending and get a Financial Health Score plus a forecast of where the month will land.

    No bank linking
  • DebtTracker

    See what a little extra each month does

    Slide in an extra payment and watch your debt-free date and the interest you save update instantly.

    No bank login
  • DocSnapper

    Sign and redact before you send

    Add your signature, permanently hide sensitive details and export the result in the format you need.

    PDF, PNG, JPG & TXT
  • Cocktale

    Keep an eye on what you drink

    Log your drinks in a personal tracker and find alcohol-free ideas for nights you'd rather skip it.

    Personal drink tracker
  • FlirtWise

    Get unstuck on a reply

    Paste the chat or add screenshots, pick a vibe and choose from ready-to-send replies.

    18 languages
  • WealthWise

    Find the subscriptions you forgot

    WealthWise spots charges that quietly repeat and shows what each one really costs per year.

    No account needed
