About PawPass

PawPass creates a simple, shareable care passport for your dog, cat or any pet, so family, friends, pet sitters, dog walkers and boarding facilities know exactly what to do.

Feeding routines, medications, vet details, emergency contacts, behavior notes and house rules are kept together and easy to find, so your pet's routine doesn't get lost when someone else is looking after them.

  • Live link or printable PDF
  • No app needed to view
  • One-time purchase, no subscription
  • 9 languages

What you can do

  • Feeding schedule

    Feeding times and portions, written down once so every caregiver gets it right.

  • Medications

    Doses and timing for every medication, alongside allergies and special care instructions.

  • Vet and emergency contacts

    Your veterinarian, clinic details and emergency contacts, easy to find when it matters.

  • Routines and house rules

    Daily routines, behavior and personality notes, and the rules of your home.

  • Share a live link

    Send a web link anyone can open with no app needed. Edit anytime and the link updates automatically.

  • Printable PDF

    Export a clean, printable PDF of your pet's care passport to print or send.

  • One passport per pet

    Create a PawPass for each of your pets, so every caregiver knows exactly who needs what.

  • Pay once, yours forever

    Unlock PawPass Lifetime with a single one-time purchase. No subscription and no recurring charges.
