About FlirtWise

FlirtWise is your AI wingman for dating apps. It helps you know exactly what to say, whoever you're talking to.

Stuck on a reply? Staring at a profile with no idea how to start? Paste a chat or drop in a screenshot and FlirtWise gives you three ready-to-send messages in the vibe you choose.

  • 3 free AI replies to start
  • Screenshots read on your device
  • 18 languages
  • Built for everyone

What you can do

Reply

Paste a conversation or upload screenshots and get 3 natural replies. Pick a vibe, copy your favorite, or ask for more like it.

Openers

Turn a profile, bio or prompt into 3 openers that beat "hey", each with a note on why it works.

Profile glow-up

Get a 0–100 score for your bio and prompts, 3 clear fixes and a rewritten version you can use right away.

Match actions

Save matches, then reply, revive a quiet chat, ask them out or get date ideas, all kept in that match's history.

AI wingman chat

Keep a running conversation with your wingman, decode a confusing message and get quick reply options.

Guides and tips

21 short guides and 60 practical tips on openers, conversation flow, asking out, confidence and respect.

Built for everyone

Men, women, non-binary and LGBTQ+ users alike. It respects pronouns and adapts to each dating app's culture.

Your data, your choice

FlirtWise asks before any text is sent to an AI provider, and you can turn AI processing off at any time.
