About DocSnapper

DocSnapper turns your iPhone into a full document scanner. Point the camera at a page and DocSnapper finds the edges, flattens the perspective and cleans up the image, then lets you sign it, redact it, run text recognition on it and export a sharp PDF.

It's built entirely on Apple's own Vision and VisionKit frameworks. Your documents are scanned, processed and stored on your device.

  • Processed on your device
  • 18 cleanup filters
  • PDF, PNG, JPG & TXT export
  • 10+ languages

What you can do

  • Scan and import

    Multi-page capture with automatic edge detection, auto-crop and perspective correction. Import photos, PDFs and images from Files.

  • 18 cleanup filters

    Magic Color, Deep B&W, De-shadow, Whiteboard, Denoise, Sharpen and more, tuned for paper, receipts, whiteboards and handwriting.

  • A real editor

    Sign, mark up, permanently hide sensitive info, add form marks, stamps and watermarks, change page size and fix curved pages.

  • Text recognition (OCR)

    Pull the text out of any scan, read it full screen, copy or share it, or export a whole folder as a text file.

  • Replace text in a scan

    Swap individual words in place. The patch matches the paper and ink color so the correction blends into the page.

  • Stay organized

    Folders with search, sorting and drag to reorder. Every edit is non-destructive, so your original scan is always kept.

  • Export and share

    PDF, PNG, JPG and TXT with compression controls and a live file-size estimate. Send to Files, Mail, Dropbox or any app.

  • iCloud sync

    Your folders follow you to every device signed in to the same Apple Account, through your own private iCloud Drive.
