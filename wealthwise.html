About WealthWise

Master your money and achieve your financial goals with WealthWise, the simple, private, all-in-one budget and expense tracker that works entirely on your device.

Whether you're saving for a goal, trying to cut spending, or simply want to understand where your money goes, WealthWise gives you the tools to take control.

  • No account required
  • No bank linking
  • Data stays on your device
  • iCloud sync across devices

What you can do

  • Expense logging

    Log unlimited transactions and see a clear monthly overview of where your money goes.

  • Financial Health Score

    One number tracking savings rate, budget discipline, spending steadiness and income, showing what's holding you back.

  • Month-end forecast

    See where your spending lands before the month is over, including recurring bills that haven't been charged yet.

  • What Changed

    Spots when a category runs hot, a charge is unusually large, or something new appears, with a next step attached.

  • Subscription audit

    Finds charges that quietly repeat, even ones you never labelled, and shows what each really costs per year.

  • Goal planner

    Set a savings target and instantly see whether it's realistic, plus a plan for exactly where the money comes from.

  • Ask WealthWise

    Ask any question about your spending in plain English and get answers from your real transactions.

  • Reports and export

    Full monthly reports, 6-month trends, bill reminders, home screen widgets and CSV or PDF export with WealthWise PRO.
